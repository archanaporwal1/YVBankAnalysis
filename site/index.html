How it works

01

Drop your statements

Text PDFs are read directly by your browser's own PDF engine. Scans and photos go through OCR on your device. Excel and CSV are read as tables.

02

It reads and checks, right here

The engine finds the transaction table, columns and date format by itself, then checks every row against the running balance so nothing slips through.

03

Download the report

Pick the account, bank and period, then save a multi-sheet Excel report with monthly analysis, risk insights, FOIR, cheque returns, cash and EMIs.

Why analyse locally

Nothing to intercept

Bank statements are among the most sensitive documents you own. Here they stay on your machine instead of passing through a third party's server.

No upload queue or file cap

Processing happens in your browser's memory, so a year of statements isn't rationed by an upload limit or a paywall.

Passwords stay with you

If a PDF is locked, the password is used only inside this tab to open it. It is never sent anywhere.
